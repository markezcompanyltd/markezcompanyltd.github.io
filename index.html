Steps to Buy our EV

Step One

Find the right model

Browse our curated selection of electric vehicles and evaluate their range, dimensions, and key features—empowering you to choose the model that best aligns with your lifestyle, preferences, and driving needs.

Step Two

Choose a payment plan

Choose a plan that fits your lifestyle and financial goals—opt for flexible monthly payments, a long-term lease, or a bundled package that includes a home EV charger for added convenience and value.

Step Three

Schedule a test drive

Schedule a test drive at a convenient location or request a personalized home demo to explore the EV’s performance, comfort, and smart features. It’s the perfect way to discover how electric mobility fits seamlessly into your lifestyle.

Step Four

Delivery & setup

We take care of everything—from delivering your EV to your doorstep, to professionally installing your home charging station if selected. Our onboarding support ensures you’re fully equipped to drive, charge, and maintain your vehicle with confidence from day one.

About UMAC Mobility

UMAC is an innovative mobility enterprise launching an integrated platform to synergize EV leasing and financing with the deployment of strategically situated charging stations across West Africa. As the region confronts escalating fuel costs and environmental challenges, we present a transformative solution: affordable access to electric vehicles complemented by reliable charging infrastructure.

To make electric vehicle adoption seamless, affordable, and sustainable across West Africa by providing innovative leasing and financing solutions, supported by a growing network of smart, reliable charging stations.
