Driving West Africa's Sustainable Future

UMAC Mobility is pioneering an integrated platform to make electric vehicles accessible, affordable, and sustainable across the region.

Our Mission & Vision

We are dedicated to revolutionizing transportation by tackling the core challenges of cost and infrastructure, empowering a seamless transition to clean energy for all.

Our Mission

To make electric vehicle adoption seamless, affordable, and sustainable across West Africa by providing innovative leasing and financing solutions, supported by a growing network of smart, reliable charging stations.

Our Vision

To become West Africa’s foremost integrated mobility provider by 2030, advancing the region’s sustainable transportation revolution through financial innovation, infrastructure development, and community-centric solutions.

The Challenge We Address

Rapid urbanization in West Africa has led to a transportation crisis defined by high costs, pollution, and limited access. We see these challenges as opportunities for transformative change.

High Upfront Costs

EVs in Ghana cost GHS 180,000-300,000, making them inaccessible for the average consumer.

Lack of Infrastructure

Fewer than 60 public chargers exist in Ghana, creating significant range anxiety for drivers.

Limited Financing

Less than 12% of adults have access to auto loans, with no specific products designed for EVs.

Environmental Impact

Aging, polluting vehicles contribute to poor air quality and significant CO₂ emissions.

Our Integrated Three-Component Solution

UMAC's innovative business model directly tackles the barriers to EV adoption by synergizing vehicles, charging, and financing into one seamless platform.

EV Sales & Leasing

Making ownership accessible through affordable, flexible models like monthly leasing and lease-to-own programs for individuals and fleets.

Charging Stations

Building a dependable, scalable network of public and private charging stations, including solar-powered options to ensure off-grid reliability.

Accessible Financing

Providing inclusive, low-interest financing plans for both vehicles and charging infrastructure, using alternative credit scoring to serve more people.

Our Positive Contributions

Our strategy is designed to deliver measurable benefits across environmental, economic, social, and technological spheres.

Environmental

An annual reduction of up to 50,000 tons of CO₂, improving air quality and creating quieter urban environments.

Economic

Generating new jobs, decreasing national fuel imports, and reducing operational costs for businesses.

Social

Providing equitable access to clean mobility, enhancing public health, and empowering youth and drivers.

Technological

Implementing innovative smart charging systems, digital financial services, and data-driven urban planning.
